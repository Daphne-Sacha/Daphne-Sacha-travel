About

Daphne & Sacha

We are passionate creators specializing in digital content, photography, drone videos and travel.

Over the past two years, we've collaborated with more than 30 hotels, beach clubs, and restaurants worldwide.

Our expertise lies in producing visually stunning, high-quality content tailored to enhance each property's social media presence, websites, and Booking/Airbnb listings. Capturing authentic moments that showcase the true essence of the hotel experience. Our photos are designed to create relatable settings, allowing potential guests to easily picture themselves enjoying their stay.

We highlight each hotel's unique atmosphere, from cozy interiors to stunning outdoor views. Our images use real-life scenarios to help guests imagine their perfect vacation, whether by the pool, dining, or waking up to a beautiful sunrise.

Services

What we create.

Content Creation

Custom digital content built for your own channels — Instagram reels, TikTok edits and website-ready visuals. Every piece is shot on-brand and designed to hold attention, tell a compelling story, and help your property stand out across platforms.

Photoshoots

Available as creators and as models — particularly for content built around a couple. Working both behind and in front of the camera, we know how to direct a scene from either side of it: romantic getaways, lifestyle moments, or a brand built on connection.

Drone Content

Cinematic aerial footage that shows a property the way guests never see it — the roofline, the coastline, the full shape of the place from above. A perspective that makes any marketing strategy feel a little more expansive.
